# Traffic Signal Operator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Traffic Signal Operator (app ID `com.tinkusgames.trafficsignaloperator`) is made by **Tinku's Games**.
This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, logins, purchases, chat or leaderboards.

## Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, best scores and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them,
and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud,
Google may collect and process:

- device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version),
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location,
- how you interact with ads, and
- diagnostics and crash information.

All ads in this game are **non-personalised**. The game does not use the Android advertising ID: the
advertising ID permission is removed from the app. Google's use of data is described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Permissions

- **Internet** and **network state**: to load ads.
- **Wake lock** and **foreground service**: added by the Google Mobile Ads library for showing ads.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

## Security

Data sent to and from Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If the game adds analytics, logins, purchases or leaderboards, we will update this policy and the date above.
