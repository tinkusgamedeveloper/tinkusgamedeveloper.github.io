# Privacy Policy: Tilchatta Chalo

*Last updated: 28 October 2026 · App: Tilchatta Chalo: Dilli Darbar (com.tilchatta.chalo)*

Tilchatta Chalo ("the app") is a game made by Tinku's Games ("we", "us"). In short: **we do not collect your personal information**. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service needs in order to show ads.

## 1. Information we collect
We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any account information. The app has no login and no user accounts.

## 2. Information stored on your device
Your game progress (scores, coins, achievements, skins, settings and language) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software may automatically collect and process: device and app information (for example device model, operating system, app version and an app-set identifier); your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location; and ad interactions and app diagnostics (for example crash and performance data). We request non-personalised ads and have removed the app's access to the Android advertising ID. Google handles this data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Sharing your score
If you tap "Share", the app creates a score image and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

## 5. Optional news headlines
The app may download a small file of news headlines for the in-game ticker. This request sends no personal information.

## 6. Permissions
The app uses internet and network-state access (for ads and headlines), plus wake-lock and foreground-service permissions that come with the ads software. It does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location.

## 7. Children
The app is political satire intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Data security and deletion
Data handled by Google's ad service is encrypted in transit. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove your game data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

## 9. Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
