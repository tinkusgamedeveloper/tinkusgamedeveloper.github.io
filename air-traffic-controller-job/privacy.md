# Air Traffic Controller Job — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

Air Traffic Controller Job (app ID `com.tinkusgames.airtrafficcontrollerjob`) is a game made by
**Tinku's Games**. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## What we collect

**Nothing personal.** The game has no account, no login and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name,
email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run our own servers that
receive data from the app.

## What stays on your phone

Your career progress, best scores, statistics and settings (such as sound and map or radar view)
are saved only on your device, inside the app. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or
clearing its data deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from **Google AdMob**: a banner on the menu screens, an occasional
full-screen ad between games, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue a shift.

- **Ads are non-personalised.** Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads only, so ads are not
  based on your interests or past activity.
- **The advertising ID is not used.** The app removes the Android advertising ID permission, so it
  cannot read your device's advertising ID.
- To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect: device and app information
  (such as device model, operating system version and app version), IP address or approximate
  location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostics such as crash logs.
- This data is collected and processed by Google under the
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also
  [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- Data is encrypted in transit.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | To load ads. The game itself works without a connection. |
| Network state | Added by the ads SDK, to check for a connection before loading an ad. |
| Wake lock, foreground service | Added by the ads SDK for its own background work. |

The app keeps the screen awake while you play using a display setting, not a permission. It does
not use the camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location permissions, and it has no sharing
feature.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from
children.

## Changes

If the game ever adds analytics, logins, purchases or online leaderboards, this policy will be
updated first, with a new date at the top.
