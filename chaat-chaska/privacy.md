# Chaat Chaska: Street Food Rush – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 October 2026*

**Chaat Chaska: Street Food Rush** ("the game", app ID `com.tinkusgames.chaatchaska`) is made by **Tinku's Games**.
This policy explains what information the game handles.

## Information we collect
**We do not collect any personal information.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases,
no analytics of our own and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

## Information stored on your device
Your progress (coins, gems, stars, upgrades, settings and language) is saved **only on your device**.
It never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game, or "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**: a banner on menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between levels,
and an optional video you can choose to watch for double coins.

- **All ads are non-personalised.** We ask AdMob for non-personalised ads only, and the game does **not** use the
  device's advertising ID (it is removed from the app).
- To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect information such as
  device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions, and crash and
  performance diagnostics. This data is **encrypted in transit**.
- Google's use of this data is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
  [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Permissions
- **Internet** and **network state** – to load ads.
- **Vibrate** – for haptic feedback (you can switch it off in Settings).
- **Keep screen awake** (wake lock) – so the screen doesn't turn off in the middle of a level.

The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

## Children
The game is **not directed at children under 13**, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example online leaderboards or purchases),
this policy will be updated before that version is released.
