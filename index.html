Tilchatta Chalo: Dilli Darbar

Lead a cockroach swarm through comic Delhi. Grow, gherao, swallow rival parties!

AndroidSwarm / ArcadeHindi & EnglishPlays offline

A funny, fast swarm game set on a comic map of Delhi. Start as one small cockroach, pick up every loose tilchatta in the city and grow into the biggest swarm in Dilli.

Features

  • Grow your swarm by recruiting loose cockroaches and eating samosas, momos, jalebi, golgappe, paratha and chole bhature.
  • Eat or be eaten: bigger swarms steal smaller ones' cockroaches. Touch a rival leader to swallow the whole party.
  • Play as one of 7 opposition parties, or switch sides and play as the Sarkar.
  • Gherao the police, and dodge lathi charges, tear gas and the water cannon.
  • Gathbandhan: team up with another party for 30 seconds.
  • A comic Delhi: India Gate, Connaught Place, Jantar Mantar, Chandni Chowk, JNU, Mukherjee Nagar, the Yamuna and Kooda Pahad. Ride the metro and dodge cows and potholes.
  • Breaking news events like paper leaks, AQI 999 smog, internet shutdowns and free bhandaras shake up every game.
  • Daily challenges with streaks, 20 achievements, Chanda coins, skins and slogan packs.
  • Share your score card on WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • Endless mode: how big can you get?

Screenshots

Political satire. All characters are fictional cockroaches. Party references are parody and are not endorsed by, or affiliated with, any political party, government body or police force.

Air Traffic Controller Job

Run a busy airport tower: land, launch and keep every plane safely apart.

AndroidSimulation / PuzzleNo login

Take the job nobody else can handle: you are the air traffic controller. Planes call in from every direction. Arrivals need a runway, departures need a gap, and one wrong call and two aircraft get far too close.

Features

  • Tap a plane and clear it to land, or simply drag it onto a runway.
  • Send departures to the runway, hold them short, then clear them for takeoff.
  • A career across four airports: Pinewood Regional, Harbor City, Meridian Hub and Summit Valley. Earn stars to unlock the next one.
  • Every shift is different: shifting wind, rain, fog, thunderstorms, emergencies and runway closures.
  • Rush hour at midday, and a sky that turns from dawn to night.
  • Grow your airport with an upgrade after each day: parking stands, faster ground crew, high-speed exits, conflict alerts and more.
  • A living airport with jet bridges, fuel trucks, baggage carts, boats and runway lights. Switch to a classic radar scope with one tap.
  • Made for phones: pinch to zoom, double-tap to see the whole airspace.

Traffic Signal Operator

Run the town's traffic lights. 22 levels of curves, flyovers and bridges.

AndroidPuzzle / SimulationPlays offline

Take charge of the traffic lights in a busy little town. Tap a signal to switch it, swap a whole junction safely, or link several signals so they change together. Keep the cars moving, and don't keep anyone waiting too long.

Features

  • 22 levels with crossroads, T-junctions, curves, flyovers, rivers and bridges.
  • Cars that turn, signal, give way and lose patience.
  • Ambulances that must never be held up.
  • Buses at their bays and people waiting to cross.
  • Protected left-turn arrows, roadworks, rain and night.
  • Linked signal groups with automatic timers.
  • Star ratings, an endless mode and a new city every day.
  • Speed time up to 2× or 3×.
  • Plays offline in landscape.

Chaat Chaska: Street Food Rush

Cook and serve Indian street food fast! Tikki, pani puri, chai, jalebi and more.

AndroidCooking / Time managementHindi & English

Welcome to Pandu's Kitchen, the busiest chaat stall in India! Customers line up hungry and each one shows exactly what they want on their order ticket. Cook it, add exactly the toppings they asked for, and serve it before they lose patience.

Features

  • Real Indian street food: aloo tikki chaat, samosa, jalebi, pani puri, dahi bhalla, chole bhature, masala chai, lassi, jaljeera, nimbu shikanji and gulab sharbat.
  • Toppings that grow with you: imli, dahi, sev, onion, chaat masala and more unlock as you play.
  • Travel across India: Chandni Chowk in Delhi, the ghats of Banaras and an Amritsar dhaba. Mumbai and Lucknow / Jaipur are coming soon.
  • 45 levels, each with its own goal, plus festival rush levels like Diwali Rush.
  • Upgrade the stall: bigger tawa, more plates, faster chulha, more patient customers.
  • Fun customers: students, office babus, aunty ji, uncle ji, food vloggers, tourists and the VIP neta.
  • Daily rewards, boosters and achievements.
